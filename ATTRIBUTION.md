# EARTH v1 source policy

Every uploaded audio file must be explicitly CC0-1.0/public domain at its source page and must be redistributable as a sample. Planned sources:

- Osiris Piano and VCSL Keys for piano families.
- Karoryfer Black and Green Guitars, Shinyguitar, and Black and Blue Basses for guitars and bass.
- VSCO 2 Community Edition raw WAV for brass, strings, and woodwinds.
- Karoryfer x bigcat cello for cello.
- VCSL and VSCO 2 CE raw percussion/mallet sources.

Rejected sources include Philharmonia (sample redistribution prohibited), mixed-license Discord GM instruments unless audited per file, Sonatina, University of Iowa, Salamander versions covered by the project blocklist, and non-redistributable commercial libraries.
